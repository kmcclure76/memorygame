The Dual Coding Challenge

Three quick games. Same idea, tested three ways: does pairing a word with an image help you remember it better than the word alone? Play them in order — no going back.

Game 1 of 3

Word Match

Flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs. Every card is a word only — no images. You have 90 seconds. Find as many pairs as you can before time runs out.

Game 1: Word Match Matches: 0/12 1:30
Game 2 of 3

Word + Image Match

Same game — but this time every card shows the word and an icon together. Find the two matching dual-coded cards. Again, 90 seconds on the clock.

Game 2: Word + Image Matches: 0/12 1:30
Game 3 of 3

Recall Round

You'll see a short list for 20 seconds. Then it disappears, and you'll type everything you remember. You'll do this twice — once with text only, once with text plus an icon. This part isn't scored — it's for you to feel the difference.

Round A: Text Only 0:20

    Round A — What do you remember?

    Type everything you can recall from the list, in any order.
